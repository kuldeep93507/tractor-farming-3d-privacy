Privacy Policy

App: Indian Tractor Farming 3D (package com.mmb.tractorfarming3d)
Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary

This game does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email or contact details. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The farmer name, avatar and country you choose, your coins, unlocked tractors, level progress and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions, crash logs and diagnostics. This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data and we do not sell personal information.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Consent in Europe, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads that use personal data, using Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" in the game's settings.

Data we collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves. The only data collected is by the Google AdMob SDK as described above. The app has no accounts and no other analytics or tracking.

Permissions

The app uses the device motion sensor only for the optional "tilt" steering mode, and vibration for crash feedback. Sensor readings are used live in the game and are not stored or transmitted.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests use Google's content and consent settings appropriate to the user's age and region.
